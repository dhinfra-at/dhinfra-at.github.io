CRASH, the Cultural Heritage Advanced Search Hackathon, takes place in Graz on 30 November and 1 December 2026. For two days, teams build search prototypes on cultural heritage data and test them. Participation is free, and registration is open until 1 November at [crash-this.github.io](https://crash-this.github.io/).

## Built on the DHinfra cluster

The teams work on the [DHinfra cluster](/gpu-cluster). They call large open-weight language models, vision models and embedding models through the OpenAI-compatible API, and they run their own jobs on NVIDIA H200, RTX PRO 6000 and L40S GPUs. Their data stays inside the academic network. Every participant gets a cluster account and the documentation on 23 November, one week before the start.

## Vibes and Checks

With current AI tools, a team can build a search application in an afternoon. At CRASH, each team also picks one area in which it tests its tool: efficiency, trust, control, access or discovery. At the end, each team shows its check. The audience chooses the winner of hearts, and a jury chooses the winner of numbers.

## Who can come

CRASH is open to students, researchers and practitioners from universities, GLAM institutions and industry. Coding skills help, but a dataset or a problem you know well is just as welcome, and you can register without a team. Teams bring their own data or work on a prepared dataset of illuminated charters with ground truth, or on the Kulturpool API. Everybody can ask for travel support in the registration.

## Programme

The hackathon runs from Monday 09:00 to Tuesday 17:00 at the UNICORN Startup & Innovation Hub in Graz. Two impulse talks frame the first day: at 10:00 on what people who work with cultural heritage hope AI can do for finding material, and at 20:00 on the methods and tools of search. Both talks are streamed. On Tuesday afternoon the teams present their results.

The schedule, the full call and the registration are at [crash-this.github.io](https://crash-this.github.io/). Questions go to [crash.organizers@gmail.com](mailto:crash.organizers@gmail.com).

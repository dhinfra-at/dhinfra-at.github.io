Until now, signing in to the [DHinfra cluster](/gpu-cluster) needed an institutional account through eduID.at. People without such an account can now get a local account.

## How it works

![Four steps to a local account: your project lead writes to us, or you write to us yourself; get an invitation by mail, valid for 7 days; sign up with a password, an authenticator app and 10 backup codes; the account is switched on after our check.](/images/posts/2026-11-26-local-accounts/local-accounts-steps.png)

1. If you want to join an existing project, contact its project lead first. The project lead writes to [dhinfra@uni-graz.at](mailto:dhinfra@uni-graz.at) with your name, institution and mail address. If you start on your own, write to us yourself and say what you want to work on.
2. You get a personal invitation by mail. It works only for your address and is valid for seven days.
3. When you sign up, you enter your name, institution and a password, connect an authenticator app on your phone or computer, and save ten backup codes.
4. We check the sign-up and switch the account on. If the invitation names a project, you are a member of it from the start.

A local account has the same rights as an eduID account: the LLM API, JupyterHub notebooks, and Slurm jobs over SSH. For SSH you add a public key, and the authenticator app gives the second factor.

If you already have an eduID account, keep signing in with it. Questions go to [dhinfra@uni-graz.at](mailto:dhinfra@uni-graz.at).
